This is a practical draft, not legal advice. A qualified professional should review it for the business, technology, and jurisdictions involved before publication.

1. Overview

Kovexa Studio Ltd. is responsible for information submitted through this site for project, service, advertising, collaboration, and general enquiries. This policy should be read together with the Terms of Service and Cookie Policy.

2. Information we collect

The contact form may collect your name, email address, company or project name, inquiry type, service interest, product stage, expected timeline, message, source page, consent state, and basic submission metadata. We also receive information you choose to include in the message.

Technical information

Hosting infrastructure may process standard request data such as an IP address, browser details, date, time, and security logs. The current site does not intentionally use advertising trackers.

3. How information is used

  • To review and respond to your request.
  • To understand the requested product, service, or collaboration context.
  • To protect the website and contact channel from misuse.
  • To keep records required for legitimate business and legal purposes.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by hosting, email, security, or professional service providers needed to operate the website and respond. It should not be sold. Any production providers and international transfer safeguards must be documented before publication.

5. Retention

Enquiry information should be kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a potential or active business relationship, protect legitimate interests, and meet legal obligations. Specific production retention periods must be confirmed.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability, and to withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing. Identity may need to be verified before a request is completed.

7. Security

Reasonable organisational and technical measures should be used to protect information. No method of internet transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to hello@kovexastudio.com. The temporary business address is 24 Northbridge Avenue, Manchester, M1 4AX, United Kingdom.
