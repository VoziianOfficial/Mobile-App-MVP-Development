Delivery standards

Quality is visible in the ordinary states.

Logical structure, keyboard operation, labels, focus, touch targets, and readable contrast.

Layouts adapt intentionally across desktop, tablet, and mobile rather than simply shrinking.

Clear components, consistent states, and practical separation of shared and page-specific behaviour.

Functional checks, required assets, environment configuration, and a support path are prepared before release.
